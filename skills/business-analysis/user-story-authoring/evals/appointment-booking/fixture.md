# Stakeholder brief — online appointment booking

We run a small network of physiotherapy clinics. Right now patients call the
front desk to book, reschedule, or cancel appointments, and the desk is
overwhelmed at peak times. We want patients to be able to manage their own
appointments online.

Notes from the stakeholder call:
- Patients should be able to see available slots and book one.
- They should be able to reschedule or cancel a booking they already have.
- A patient who cancels late (less than 24 hours before) may be charged a fee —
  finance still has to confirm the exact policy.
- Front-desk staff still need to be able to book on a patient's behalf over the phone.
- We were also asked about SMS reminders, but that may be a later phase.
- Patients must be signed in to manage appointments.
