# Opportunity Candidate Template

Provisional value-area interpretation over evidence — not an intake record, not a solution.
May carry an incomplete product advantage. Store in `opportunities/candidates/`.

```yaml
---
title: "Short opportunity candidate title"
status: active | deferred | expired
confidence: weak | moderate | strong
created: "YYYY-MM-DD"
last_reviewed: "YYYY-MM-DD"
review_trigger: "What should cause this candidate to be reviewed again?"
---
```

## Current Interpretation

Possible value area. 1-2 sentences.

## Incomplete Product Advantage

The emerging value-creating direction. Can be incomplete at candidate stage. Note any of:
beneficiary, comparison point, advantage type, timing rationale, validation path.

## Linked Problem Briefs Or Strategic Thesis

Problem briefs, problem candidates, strategic theses, or signals that justify this.

-

## Affected Outcomes

Outcome links/names. Use `unknown` if unclear.

## Affected Personas / Clients

Personas, clients, accounts, segments. Known only.

## Evidence References

Radar signals, docs, source systems, evidence handles.

- `path-or-url`

## Decision Assumptions

Local assumptions behind the candidate.

-

## Remaining Uncertainty

Decision-changing uncertainty. If none, write `None identified` and why the evidence is enough
for the next decision.

## Discovery Questions

Only questions whose answer changes the next product decision. If none, write `None identified`.

-

## Review Notes

Brief review history and local decisions.
